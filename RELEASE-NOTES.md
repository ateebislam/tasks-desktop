Workspaces, buckets, new views, archive and Trash.
- Product and Marketing workspaces, each with its own tasks, statuses and automations. Switch at the top left.
- A Bucket column (what the work is for) next to Platform (where it happens). Click any column name to rename it.
- Three views: Table, List (grouped, by platform to start) and Board, where you drag cards to change status.
- Finished tasks move to the Archive after 30 days. Deleted tasks wait 15 days in the Trash. Documents are never deleted automatically.
- New Buckets and Documents pages, with a document preview.
